Site Name

ExchangeHub

ExchangeHub was choosen because it prepresentsa a place where people can exchange useful items such as clothes, books,jewelry, electronics and household goods with other users.

Site Purpose

ExchangeHub allows users to exchange items instead of throwing them away or buying new ones.Users can put items for listing and trade it with another user item. The website encourages recycling,sustanability and saving money.
